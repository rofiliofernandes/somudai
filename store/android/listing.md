## Full description (4000)
Somudai makes it easier to take part in public life near you.

Find matters around you: beach cleanups, school drives, civic meetings and
public questions that affect your area. When something matters to you, Tap:

• "I'm joining" for actions you plan to be part of
• "I stand with this" for stances you support

Every number on Somudai is a real person. Counts show only totals: who tapped
a post is never shown publicly, and your Taps are never sold or shared.

Somudai is quiet by design. No likes, no comments, no trending, no endless
feeds. One clear action per post, with the count beside it.

Somudai hosts civic and political matters but never takes sides.

Starting in Goa.
